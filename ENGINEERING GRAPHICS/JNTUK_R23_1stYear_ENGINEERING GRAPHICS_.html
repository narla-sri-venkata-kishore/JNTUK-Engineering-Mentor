// SYLLABUS

Unit-wise Content

UNIT I
Basics, Curves & Scales
▼
  • Drawing standards, lines, lettering & dimensioning
  • Geometrical constructions & regular polygons
  • Conic sections: Ellipse, Parabola, Hyperbola
  • Special curves: Cycloid, Epicycloid, Hypocycloid
  • Involute of circle and polygon
  • Tangents & Normals to curves
  • Scales: Plain, Diagonal, Vernier

🎯 EXAM TIPS

  • Curves are VERY frequently asked — practice all methods
  • Scale problems are scoring — learn formulae
  • Show all construction steps clearly with arcs and marks
DifficultyMedium
UNIT II
Orthographic Projections
▼
  • Reference planes: HP (Horizontal Plane) & VP (Vertical Plane)
  • Four quadrants — 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th angle projections
  • Projection of points in all quadrants
  • Projection of straight lines — parallel to both planes
  • Lines perpendicular to one plane
  • Lines inclined to one plane (HP or VP)
  • Lines inclined to both planes (True length & inclinations)
  • Projection of planes (triangular, rectangular, circular)

🎯 EXAM TIPS

  • Line inclinations to both planes — VERY important
  • Quadrant problems are repeated every year
  • Always draw the XY reference line first
DifficultyHigh
UNIT III
Projections of Solids
▼
  • Types: Polyhedra (Prism, Pyramid, Tetrahedron) & Solids of revolution
  • Axis perpendicular to HP — base on HP
  • Axis perpendicular to VP — base on VP
  • Axis parallel to both HP & VP
  • Axis inclined to HP (change of position method)
  • Axis inclined to VP
  • Axis inclined to both reference planes

🎯 EXAM TIPS

  • Inclined axis solids — HIGH weightage, practice daily
  • Always draw all visible & hidden edges
  • Use pencil H/2H for construction, HB for outlines
DifficultyVery High
UNIT IV
Sections & Development of Surfaces
▼
  • Section planes: perpendicular & inclined
  • Sectional front view, top view & side view
  • True shape of section (auxiliary view)
  • Development of cube & rectangular prism
  • Development of cylinder (lateral surface)
  • Development of pyramid & cone
  • Transition pieces and truncated solids

🎯 EXAM TIPS

  • Development is a SCORING topic — practice flat patterns
  • Draw accurate shapes, label all points A, B, C...
  • Always find true shape of section using auxiliary view
DifficultyMedium-High
UNIT V
Conversion of Views & Computer Graphics
▼
  • Isometric projections & isometric views
  • Isometric scale (0.816 × true length)
  • Conversion: Isometric → Orthographic views
  • Conversion: Orthographic → Isometric views
  • Introduction to Computer Graphics
  • AutoCAD: 2D drafting — LINE, CIRCLE, ARC, TRIM, OFFSET
  • AutoCAD: 3D modeling — EXTRUDE, REVOLVE, UNION

🎯 EXAM TIPS

  • Conversion questions are EASY marks — don't skip!
  • Remember isometric axes are at 30° to horizontal
  • Practice AutoCAD commands for theory questions
DifficultyMedium

Key Formulae & Rules

Orthographic Rules
1st angle: object between observer and plane. 3rd angle: plane between observer and object.
In 1st angle projection, Front view is placed BELOW the top view and to the LEFT of the side view.
Isometric Angles
Three axes at 120° to each other. Horizontal axes at 30° to the horizontal line.
Isometric length = True length × 0.816 (cos 30°). For isometric VIEW use true lengths directly.
Scale Formulae
RF = Drawing Length ÷ Actual Length. Diagonal scale reads 3 units of measurement.
RF < 1 = Reducing scale | RF = 1 = Full size | RF > 1 = Enlarging scale
Section Rules
Section lines are drawn at 45° to the outline, equally spaced. True shape found by auxiliary view.
When section plane is perpendicular to HP and inclined to VP → True shape appears in SIDE VIEW.
Development Methods
Parallel line method: prisms & cylinders. Radial line method: pyramids & cones.
Slant height of cone = √(r² + h²). Arc length of development = 2πr × (slant height/slant height).
Curve Construction
Ellipse: Sum of distances from two foci is constant. Cycloid: circle rolls on straight line.
Parabola: locus of a point equidistant from focus and directrix. Used in parabolic reflectors & bridges.

Score Maximum Marks

  • Practice 2 drawings daily without reference
  • Focus on neatness — examiners notice it first
  • Memorize step-by-step construction sequences
  • Revise all units at least twice
  • Prepare a formula card for quick revision

📝 DURING EXAM

  • Read all questions before starting
  • Use H pencil for construction, HB for outlines
  • Use proper dimensioning and title block
  • Maintain consistent line quality throughout
  • Label all points (A, B, C...) and views clearly
