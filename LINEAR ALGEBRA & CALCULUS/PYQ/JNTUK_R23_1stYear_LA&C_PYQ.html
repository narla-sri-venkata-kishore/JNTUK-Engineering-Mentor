Here v = x/u, i.e. x = uv → explicit functional relation exists.
IV.5.10 Find the extreme values of f(x,y)=\sin x + \sin y + \sin(x+y) for x,y \in [0,π/2].
Medium Occasionally Asked
Critical: f_x=\cos x+\cos(x+y)=0, f_y=\cos y+\cos(x+y)=0.
Subtract: \cos x = \cos y → x=y. Then 2\cos x + \cos 2x = 0.
Solution: x=y=π/3. Max f=3√3/2.
IV.5.11 Find the point on the plane x+2y+3z=14 closest to the origin using Lagrange multipliers.
Medium Frequently Asked
Minimize f=x²+y²+z² subject to g=x+2y+3z−14=0.
∇f=λ∇g: 2x=λ, 2y=2λ, 2z=3λ. So x=λ/2, y=λ, z=3λ/2.
Substitute into constraint: λ/2+2λ+9λ/2=14 → λ=2 → (1,2,3).
IV.5.12 Expand f(x,y)=e^{x+y} and f(x,y)=\cos x \cos y about (0,0) using Taylor's series for two variables up to third-degree terms.
Hard Occasionally Asked
All partials at (0,0) for e^{x+y} equal 1.
e^{x+y} = 1+(x+y)+\frac{(x+y)^2}{2}+\frac{(x+y)^3}{6}+…
For \cos x \cos y, use product of Maclaurin expansions.
IV.5.13 Find the dimensions of a rectangular box of maximum volume inscribed in a sphere of radius R using Lagrange multipliers.
Hard Very Frequently Asked
Maximize V=xyz subject to x²+y²+z²=R².
By symmetry and Lagrange: x=y=z=R/√3.
Max volume = (R/√3)³ = R³/(3√3). The box is a cube!

V.5.01 Change the order of integration and evaluate: ∫₀¹ ∫_x^{√x} (x+y)\,dy\,dx.
Medium Very Frequently Asked

Evaluate inner then outer integral; answer = 3/10.
V.5.02 Evaluate ∬_R e^{−(x²+y²)}\,dA over the entire xy-plane using polar coordinates. Hence find ∫₋∞^∞ e^{−x²}\,dx.
Medium Very Frequently Asked
Convert: ∫₀^{2π}∫₀^∞ e^{−r²} r\,dr\,dθ = 2π · (1/2) = π.
So (∫e^{−x²}dx)² = π → ∫₋∞^∞ e^{−x²}dx = √π. The Gaussian integral!
V.5.03 Find the volume of a sphere of radius a using a triple integral in spherical coordinates.
Hard Very Frequently Asked
V = ∫₀^{2π}∫₀^π∫₀^a ρ²\sinφ\,dρ\,dφ\,dθ.
Inner: ∫₀^a ρ²dρ = a³/3. Middle: ∫₀^π \sinφ dφ = 2. Outer: ∫₀^{2π}dθ = 2π.
V = (a³/3)·2·2π = 4πa³/3 ✓.
V.5.04 Find the area bounded by the parabola y²=4ax and its latus rectum x=a using a double integral.
Medium Very Frequently Asked
Area = ∫₋{2a}^{2a} ∫_0^{y²/(4a)} dx\,dy = 2∫₀^{2a} y²/(4a)\,dy.
Evaluate: = 2·[y³/(12a)]₀^{2a} = 2·8a³/(12a) = 4a²/3.
Area = 8a²/3.
V.5.05 Evaluate ∭ xyz\,dV over the tetrahedron bounded by the planes x=0, y=0, z=0, and x+y+z=1.
Hard Frequently Asked
Limits: x: 0→1, y: 0→1−x, z: 0→1−x−y.
∫₀¹ x ∫₀^{1−x} y ∫₀^{1−x−y} z\,dz\,dy\,dx. Evaluate iteratively.
Answer = 1/720.
V.5.06 Evaluate ∬_R \sqrt{a²−x²−y²}\,dA over the circle x²+y²≤a² by changing to polar coordinates.
Hard Frequently Asked
In polar: ∫₀^{2π}∫₀^a \sqrt{a²−r²} · r\,dr\,dθ.
Let u=a²−r², du=−2r\,dr. Inner = [−(a²−r²)^{3/2}/3]₀^a = a³/3.
Answer = 2π · a³/3 = 2πa³/3 (volume of hemisphere).
V.5.07 Evaluate ∫₀^a ∫₀^{\sqrt{a²−x²}} (x²+y²)\,dy\,dx by changing to polar coordinates.
Medium Frequently Asked
Region is quarter-circle; in polar: ∫₀^{π/2}∫₀^a r²·r\,dr\,dθ.
= ∫₀^{π/2}dθ · ∫₀^a r³dr = (π/2)·(a⁴/4) = πa⁴/8.
V.5.08 Find the volume of the cone z = \sqrt{x²+y²} bounded above by the plane z=h using cylindrical coordinates.
Hard Frequently Asked
In cylindrical: z=r. Limits: θ:0→2π, r:0→h, z:r→h.
V = ∫₀^{2π}∫₀^h ∫_r^h r\,dz\,dr\,dθ = 2π ∫₀^h r(h−r)dr.
= 2π[hr²/2 − r³/3]₀^h = 2π·h³/6 = πh³/3 ✓.
V.5.09 Change the order of integration and evaluate: ∫₀^{π/2} ∫_y^{π/2} \frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx\,dy.
Medium Very Frequently Asked
Original region: y≤x≤π/2, 0≤y≤π/2. After change: 0≤y≤x, 0≤x≤π/2.
∫₀^{π/2} \frac{\sin x}{x} ∫₀^x dy\,dx = ∫₀^{π/2} \sin x\,dx.
= [−\cos x]₀^{π/2} = 1.
V.5.10 Evaluate ∭ (x²+y²+z²)\,dV over the sphere x²+y²+z²≤a² using spherical coordinates.
Hard Very Frequently Asked
In spherical: x²+y²+z²=ρ². Integral becomes ∫₀^{2π}∫₀^π∫₀^a ρ²·ρ²\sinφ\,dρ\,dφ\,dθ.
= 2π·2·(a⁵/5) = 4πa⁵/5.
V.5.11 Find the area enclosed between y=x² and y=\sqrt{x} using a double integral.
Medium Frequently Asked
Intersection: x=0,1. Area = ∫₀¹ ∫_{x²}^{√x} dy\,dx.
= ∫₀¹ (√x−x²)\,dx = [2x^{3/2}/3 − x³/3]₀¹ = 2/3 − 1/3 = 1/3.
V.5.12 Find the volume of the solid bounded by the paraboloid z=4−x²−y² and the plane z=0 using a double integral in polar form.

Region: x²+y²≤4 (circle radius 2). In polar: r≤2.
V=∫₀^{2π}∫₀^2 (4−r²)r\,dr\,dθ = 2π∫₀^2 (4r−r³)\,dr.
= 2π[2r²−r⁴/4]₀^2 = 2π(8−4) = 8π.
V.5.13 Using cylindrical coordinates, evaluate ∭ z\,dV over the region bounded by z=0, z=2, and x²+y²=1.
