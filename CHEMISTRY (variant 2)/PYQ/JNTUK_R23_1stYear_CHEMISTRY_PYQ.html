U1 · Q20 · 5M
A particle is confined in a 1-D box of length 1 nm. Calculate the energies (in eV) for the first three levels and the frequency of light emitted for n=3 → n=1 transition. (m = 9.11×10⁻³¹ kg)
Hard 🔵 Frequently Asked

Derive the Nernst equation for an electrochemical cell. Solve: Calculate the EMF of the cell Zn | Zn²⁺(0.001M) || Ag⁺(0.1M) | Ag at 25°C. Given E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) = −0.76 V and E°(Ag⁺/Ag) = +0.80 V.

State and explain Beer-Lambert's Law. Derive the expression A = εcl. Discuss its limitations and solve: a solution of concentration 2×10⁻⁴ M in a 2 cm cell has T = 0.25. Find ε.
Medium⭐ Very Frequently Asked
Beer's Law: A ∝ c; Lambert's Law: A ∝ l
Combined: A = εcl; where A = –log T = log(I₀/I)

ε = A/(cl) = 0.602/(2×10⁻⁴ × 2) = 1505 L mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹
Limitations: Valid only at low concentration; stray light; polychromatic radiation; chemical changes

Modes: Reverse-phase (most common), normal-phase, ion-exchange, size-exclusion
Applications: Pharmaceutical analysis, food safety, environmental monitoring, protein separation, forensics
U5 · Q16 · 5M
