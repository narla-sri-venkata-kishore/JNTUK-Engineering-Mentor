U1 · Q16 · 5M
Test for exactness and solve:

Step 1: M = 3x²y + y³, N = x³ + 3xy²
Step 2: ∂M/∂y = 3x² + 3y² = ∂N/∂x → Exact ✓

U1 · Q17 · 5M
A body at 80°C is placed in a room at 20°C. After 5 minutes the body cools to 60°C. Find the temperature after 15 minutes. (Apply Newton's Law of Cooling)
Step 1: T(t) = 20 + (80−20)e^(−kt) = 20 + 60e^(−kt)
Step 2: T(5) = 60 → 60 = 20 + 60e^(−5k) → e^(−5k) = 2/3
Step 3: −5k = ln(2/3) → k = (1/5)ln(3/2)
Step 4: T(15) = 20 + 60·(2/3)³ = 20 + 60·(8/27) ≈ 37.8°C
Hard ★ Very Frequently Asked
U1 · Q18 · 5M
A radioactive substance has a half-life of 1600 years. Find the quantity remaining after 800 years if 200 grams were present initially. (Law of Decay)
Step 1: N = N₀ e^(−kt), half-life: 1/2 = e^(−1600k)
Step 2: k = ln2/1600
Step 3: N(800) = 200·e^(−800·ln2/1600) = 200·e^(−ln2/2) = 200·(1/√2)
Step 4: N(800) = 200/√2 ≈ 141.4 g
