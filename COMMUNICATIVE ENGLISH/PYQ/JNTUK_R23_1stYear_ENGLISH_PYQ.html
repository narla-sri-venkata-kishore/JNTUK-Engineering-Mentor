Long Answer · 5 Marks
U1·L1
Write a paragraph (8–10 sentences) on "The Importance of Sacrifice in Relationships" drawing references from The Gift of the Magi.
MediumVery Frequently Asked
Sample Answer:
Sacrifice is the highest expression of love. In O. Henry's short story The Gift of the Magi, Jim and Della demonstrate this timeless truth through their selfless actions. Della, the young wife, possesses two treasures: her beautiful long hair and her deep love for Jim. Without hesitation, she sells her hair to buy Jim a platinum watch chain. Meanwhile, Jim sells his prized gold watch to buy Della a set of ivory combs for her hair. The tragic irony is that each gift becomes useless — the combs cannot adorn shorn hair, and the chain has no watch to hold. Yet O. Henry insists they are the wisest of givers, for their act echoes the biblical Magi. True sacrifice does not calculate gains; it flows from the heart. In relationships, sacrifice builds trust, deepens bonds, and reveals character. Jim and Della remind us that material loss is insignificant when love is the motive. Their story teaches every reader that giving without expecting anything in return is the greatest form of human connection.
U1·L2
Explain the theme of The Gift of the Magi with specific examples from the story. What moral lesson does O. Henry convey?
MediumFrequently Asked
Key Points to Cover:
1. Theme of Selfless Love – Both characters prioritize the happiness of the other over personal attachment.
2. Irony of the Gifts – Each gift, though rendered useless, is perfectly symbolic of devotion.
3. Reference to Biblical Magi – O. Henry elevates simple acts of love to the sacred.
4. Moral – True wealth is measured in the depth of love shared, not material possessions.
5. Setting – The poverty of Jim and Della's home emphasizes the enormity of their sacrifice.
Write 5–8 sentences expanding each point with evidence from the text.
