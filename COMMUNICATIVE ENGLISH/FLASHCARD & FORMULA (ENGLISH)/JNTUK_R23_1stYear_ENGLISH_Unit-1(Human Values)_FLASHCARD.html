📖 Literature — Gift of the Magi

O. Henry's classic short story on love, sacrifice, and irony

Literature
Who wrote "The Gift of the Magi" and when was it published?
🎯 Author & Backgroundtap to reveal →
Answer
  • Author: O. Henry (pen name of William Sydney Porter)
  • Published: 1905 (in The New York Sunday World)
  • Genre: Short story / Romantic fiction
  • Setting: A furnished flat in New York City, Christmas Eve
  • Narrative Style: Third-person with an intrusive narrator
📖 Background
Literature
Who are the two main characters? Describe them briefly.
🎯 Characterstap to reveal →
Answer
  • Della Young: A loving, resourceful wife; possesses beautiful long hair (her prized possession). Impulsive, emotional, caring.
  • Jim Young (James Dillingham Young): Della's husband; responsible, hard-working. Owns a gold pocket watch (his family heirloom). Earns $20/week (reduced from $30).
  • Both are young, poor, but deeply in love.
👥 Characters
Literature
Write a brief plot summary of "The Gift of the Magi."
🎯 Plottap to reveal →
Answer
  • Della has only $1.87 to buy Jim a Christmas gift.
  • She sells her hair for $20 and buys a platinum watch chain for Jim.
  • Jim comes home and is stunned — he had sold his watch to buy jewelled combs for Della's hair.
  • Both gifts are now useless, yet the story celebrates their selfless love.
  • The narrator compares them to the Biblical Magi (Wise Men), calling them the wisest gift-givers.
📋 Plot Summary
Literature
What are the key themes of "The Gift of the Magi"?
🎯 Themestap to reveal →
Answer
  • 1. Love & Sacrifice: Both characters give up their most prized possession for each other.
  • 2. Selflessness: True giving means prioritizing others over oneself.
  • 3. Irony: The gifts become useless, yet the act of giving is priceless.
  • 4. Poverty vs. Richness of Spirit: Materially poor but emotionally rich.
  • 5. True Wisdom: Wisdom lies in loving unconditionally, not in material wealth.
💡 Themes
Literature
What type of irony is used in the story? Give an example.
🎯 Ironytap to reveal →
Answer
  • Situational Irony: The outcome is opposite to what is expected.
  • Example: Della sells her hair to buy a chain for Jim's watch. Jim sells his watch to buy combs for Della's hair.
  • Each gift is meant to complement something the other person no longer has.
  • This irony deepens the story's message — love, not the gift, is what matters.
🔁 Irony
Literature
What is the significance of the title "The Gift of the Magi"?
🎯 Title Significancetap to reveal →
Answer
  • Magi = the Three Wise Men (Biblical) who brought gifts to Baby Jesus.
  • The narrator compares Della and Jim to the Magi — both gave the greatest gifts of all.
  • The title suggests that true wisdom is found in selfless love, not in material gifts.
  • Their sacrifice is the most valuable "gift," surpassing all earthly possessions.
🏷️ Title
Literature
Quote an important line from the story and explain its meaning.
🎯 Important Linestap to reveal →
Answer
Line: "Of all who give and receive gifts, such as they are the wisest."

Meaning:
  • The narrator praises Della and Jim as the wisest gift-givers.
  • Despite giving gifts that became useless, their selfless love makes them truly wise.
  • True wisdom = loving someone enough to sacrifice what matters most to you.

Listening
What is "Listening for the Topic"? How is it different from detailed listening?
🎯 Topic Identificationtap to reveal →
Answer
Listening for the Topic:
  • Identifying the main subject of what is being said.
  • You do NOT focus on every detail — just the gist.
  • Example: Hearing a radio program and identifying it is about "climate change" even without catching every word.
  • Vs Detailed Listening: Detailed = every fact; Topic = broad idea only.
🎯 Topic Listening
Listening
Define "Listening for Context." Give a real-life example.
🎯 Context Listeningtap to reveal →
Answer
Definition: Understanding the situation, background, or setting from which a conversation or speech arises.

Example:
  • Hearing someone say "Doctor, it hurts here" — you understand the context is a medical appointment.
  • Context listening uses clues like tone, vocabulary, and setting to deduce meaning.
🔍 Context Listening
Listening
What is "Listening for Specific Information"? When is it used?
🎯 Specific Infotap to reveal →
Answer
Definition: Listening to pick out particular details — names, dates, numbers, facts — from a longer speech.

When Used:
  • Listening to train announcements for departure time.
  • Listening to a weather forecast for temperature.
  • Listening in exams for answer cues.
Technique: Know what you're looking for before you listen.
📌 Specific Info
Listening
List 5 key tips to improve listening comprehension in exams.
🎯 Tipstap to reveal →
Answer
  • 1. Preview questions before listening — know what to look for.
  • 2. Focus on keywords — names, numbers, places.
  • 3. Don't panic if you miss a word — context fills gaps.
  • 4. Take notes in shorthand during the listening.
  • 5. Pay attention to tone — happy, sad, angry tones signal meaning.

Reading
What is skimming? When do you use it? Give an example.
🎯 Skimmingtap to reveal →
Answer
Skimming: Reading quickly to get the general idea / main topic without reading every word.

When to Use: When you want to decide if a text is relevant to read fully.

Example: Reading only the headings, first and last sentences of paragraphs in a newspaper article.

Key: Speed + Overview.
⚡ Skimming
Reading
What is scanning? When do you use it? Give an example.
🎯 Scanningtap to reveal →
Answer
Scanning: Moving your eyes quickly over a text to find a specific piece of information.

When to Use: When you are looking for a particular fact, name, number, or keyword.

Example: Looking for a phone number in a directory, or finding a date in a history text.

Key: Target + Locate.

Writing
What are the 5 main rules of capitalization?
🎯 Capitalizationtap to reveal →
Answer
  • 1. First word of every sentence: The cat sat.
  • 2. Proper nouns: Names of people, places, months — Della, New York, Monday.
  • 3. Pronoun "I" is always capitalized.
  • 4. Titles before names: Dr. Smith, Professor Ray.
  • 5. First word of a quote: He said, "Come here."
🔠 Capitalization
Writing
What are the key punctuation marks and their uses?
🎯 Punctuationtap to reveal →
Answer
  • Period (.): End of a statement. She left.
  • Comma (,): Pause in a sentence or list. Della, Jim, and O. Henry.
  • Question Mark (?): End of a question. Did she sell her hair?
  • Exclamation (!): Emotion or emphasis. What a gift!
  • Apostrophe ('): Possession or contraction. Jim's watch; didn't.
  • Quotation Marks (""): Direct speech. "I love you," she said.
⁉️ Punctuation
Writing
What are common spelling rules in English?
🎯 Spelling Rulestap to reveal →
Answer
  • I before E except after C: believe, receive.
  • Drop silent 'e' before vowel suffix: love → loving.
  • Double consonant before -ing/-ed (short vowel): run → running.
  • Change Y to I before suffix (consonant + y): happy → happiness.
  • Plural: -s or -es: cat → cats; bush → bushes.
✅ Spelling
Writing
What are the basic types of sentences by structure?
🎯 Sentence Typestap to reveal →
Answer
  • Simple: One subject + one predicate. Della sold her hair.
  • Compound: Two independent clauses joined by and/but/or. Jim sold his watch, and Della sold her hair.
  • Complex: One independent + one dependent clause. Although she was poor, she bought him a gift.
  • Compound-Complex: Multiple clauses mixed. More advanced writing.

Writing
What is a topic sentence? Why is it important in a paragraph?
🎯 Topic Sentencetap to reveal →
Answer
Topic Sentence: The sentence that states the main idea of a paragraph. Usually the first sentence.

Importance:
  • Guides the reader on what to expect.
  • Keeps the paragraph focused on one idea.
  • All other sentences must support it.
Example: "Della's love for Jim is the central theme of the story."
📌 Topic Sentence
Writing
What is the correct paragraph structure?
🎯 Paragraph Structuretap to reveal →
Answer
  • 1. Topic Sentence: Main idea of the paragraph.
  • 2. Supporting Sentences: Details, examples, evidence.
  • 3. Concluding Sentence: Wraps up / reinforces the main idea.
Remember: One paragraph = One idea. Never mix multiple unrelated ideas.
📋 Paragraph
Writing

Answer

  • 2. Wrong verb tense — mixing past and present.
  • 3. Spelling errors — "recieve" instead of "receive."
  • 4. Missing capital letters — not capitalizing proper nouns.
  • 5. Subject-verb disagreement — "She have" instead of "She has."

Grammar
How do you form a Yes/No question in English?
🎯 Question Formationtap to reveal →
Answer
Rule: Auxiliary verb + Subject + Main verb + ?

Examples:
  • She sold her hair. → Did she sell her hair?
  • He is reading. → Is he reading?
  • They have left. → Have they left?
Auxiliaries: do/does/did, is/am/are, has/have/had, will, can, should.

🎯 WH Questionstap to reveal →
Answer

WH words: Who, What, Where, When, Why, How, Which

Examples:
  • What did Della sell?
  • Where did she go?
  • Why did Jim sell his watch?
  • How much did she get?

What does "Magi" mean? Why are Della and Jim compared to them?
🎯 Title Wordtap to reveal →
Answer
Magi: The Three Wise Men (from the Bible) who brought gifts to Baby Jesus at Christmas.

Why Compared?
  • Just like the Magi gave meaningful, sacrificial gifts, Della and Jim gave the most valuable thing they owned.
  • The narrator calls them "the wisest" gift-givers of all.
🌟 Title Word
Glossary
What is a "platinum fob chain"? Why did Della buy it?
🎯 Story Itemtap to reveal →
Answer
Platinum Fob Chain: A chain made of platinum (a precious metal) used to attach and display a pocket watch.

Why Della bought it:
  • Jim's gold watch was a prized possession but had a worn-out strap.
  • Della wanted to give him a worthy chain to display it proudly.
  • She paid $21 for it — more than she earned by selling her hair.

Glossary
What are "jewelled combs"? Why did Jim buy them for Della?
🎯 Story Itemtap to reveal →
Answer
Jewelled Combs: Decorative hair combs adorned with precious jewels — a luxury hair accessory.

Why Jim bought them:
  • Della's long, beautiful hair was her greatest treasure.
  • Jim had admired combs displayed in a shop window that he knew Della wanted.
  • He sold his watch to buy them — making the gift heartbreakingly ironic.

What does "furnished flat" mean in the context of the story?
🎯 Settingtap to reveal →
Answer
Furnished Flat: A rented apartment that comes with basic furniture already provided.

In the Story:
  • Della and Jim live in a small furnished flat — suggesting they are financially poor.
  • The flat is described as shabby, with a broken letterbox and a doorbell that doesn't ring.
  • The setting establishes their poverty as the story's backdrop.

What does "Queen of Sheba" mean in the story?
🎯 Allusiontap to reveal →
Answer
Queen of Sheba: A Biblical queen renowned for her wealth, beauty, and wisdom.

In Story: O. Henry says that if the Queen of Sheba lived in the building below, Della would have let her hair hang out the window just to make her feel envious.

Purpose: A literary allusion to show how extraordinarily beautiful Della's hair was.

🎯 Allusiontap to reveal →

King Solomon: The Biblical king renowned for his immense wealth and treasures.

What is meant by "O. Henry's trademark twist ending"?
🎯 Literary Techniquetap to reveal →
Answer
Twist Ending: An unexpected or surprising conclusion that changes how you understand the story.

O. Henry's Style:
  • Famous for surprise endings that seem obvious in hindsight.
  • In Gift of Magi: Reader doesn't know until the end that BOTH characters made their sacrifice simultaneously.
  • The twist makes the irony and love hit harder.

What does "intrusive narrator" mean? How is it used in this story?
🎯 Narrative Styletap to reveal →
Answer
Intrusive Narrator: A narrator who interrupts the story to speak directly to the reader and share opinions.

In Story:
  • O. Henry's narrator directly addresses the reader: "Let me introduce you to Della..."
  • The narrator comments on events, adds humor, and draws conclusions.
  • Creates a warm, conversational tone throughout.

What does "$1.87" signify at the start of the story?
🎯 Story Symboltap to reveal →
Answer
$1.87: The total amount Della has saved to buy Jim a Christmas gift.

Significance:
  • It is heartbreakingly small — barely enough for anything meaningful.
  • Sets up the central conflict: she wants to give a worthy gift but lacks money.
  • Symbolizes their poverty and makes Della's eventual sacrifice more impactful.

What is the significance of Christmas in the story?
🎯 Setting/Symboltap to reveal →
Answer
  • Occasion: The story is set on Christmas Eve.
  • Christmas represents the spirit of giving, love, and generosity.
  • The Biblical Magi gave gifts at Christmas — connecting the title to the setting.
  • The holiday amplifies the meaning of sacrifice: giving even when you have little is the truest form of love.
  • Christmas symbolizes hope and renewal, even in poverty.
