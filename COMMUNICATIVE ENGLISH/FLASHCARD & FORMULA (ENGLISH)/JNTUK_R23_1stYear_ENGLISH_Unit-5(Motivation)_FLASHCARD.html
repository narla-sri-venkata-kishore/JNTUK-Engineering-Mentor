How to Use These Flashcards

These flashcards are designed to help B.Tech 1st Year students master English Unit V efficiently. Use the navigation tabs above to jump between sections.

Step-by-step study method
  • Click a card to reveal the FRONT (question), then think of your answer.
  • Click again to reveal the BACK (answer) and compare.
  • Mark cards you got wrong — revisit them after every full round.
  • Group cards by section for focused revision using the nav tabs.
  • Use the Vocabulary section to build academic word power for essays.
  • Revise the Grammar section the day before exams for error-correction questions.
