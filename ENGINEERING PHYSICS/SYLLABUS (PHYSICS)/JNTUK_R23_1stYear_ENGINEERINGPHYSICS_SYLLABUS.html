// Formula Sheet

Quick Formula Revision

Bragg's Law
2d sinθ = nλ
d = interplanar spacing, θ = glancing angle, n = order of diffraction, λ = X-ray wavelength. Used to find crystal structure.
Schrödinger Equation
-ℏ²/2m · ∂²ψ/∂x² + Vψ = Eψ
Time-independent form. ψ = wave function, E = total energy, V = potential energy, ℏ = reduced Planck's constant.
de Broglie Wavelength
λ = h/p = h/mv
Every particle has an associated wavelength. h = Planck's constant, p = momentum. Verified by Davisson-Germer experiment.
Einstein Relation
D/μ = kT/e
Relates diffusion coefficient D to mobility μ. k = Boltzmann constant, T = temperature, e = electron charge. Holds for both electrons and holes.
Clausius-Mossotti
(εr-1)/(εr+2) = Nα/3ε₀
Relates macroscopic dielectric constant εr to microscopic polarizability α. N = number density of molecules.
Newton's Rings
D²_n = 4nλR
Dn = diameter of nth dark ring, λ = wavelength, R = radius of curvature of the lens. Used in optical testing.
Particle in Box Energy
Eₙ = n²π²ℏ²/2mL²
n = quantum number (1,2,3...), L = box length, m = particle mass. Energy is quantized — no zero-point zero energy.
Hall Coefficient
RH = -1/ne (n-type)
n = carrier concentration, e = electron charge. Negative for electrons, positive for holes. Used to find carrier type and density.
Uncertainty Principle
Δx·Δp ≥ ℏ/2
Cannot simultaneously know exact position and momentum. ℏ = h/2π. Also: ΔE·Δt ≥ ℏ/2.
Interplanar Spacing
d = a/√(h²+k²+l²)
For cubic crystal. a = lattice constant, (h k l) = Miller indices. Use with Bragg's law for XRD analysis.
